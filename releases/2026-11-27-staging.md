## Scope
1. Connector secrets. Each person's connector secrets are sealed and reach only the minds that person installs them on. The key that seals them is kept on the staging host, outside the hive's data, and handed to the hive when it starts. [omegahive#449](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive/pull/449), [omegahive-deploy#99](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive-deploy/pull/99), [omegahive-deploy#100](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive-deploy/pull/100).
2. Apps by swarm. The person who owns a swarm turns apps on and off in it; an admin turns an app on only where no person owns the swarm, and off anywhere. [omegahive#751](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive/pull/751), [omegahive-ui#80](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive-ui/pull/80), [omegahive-ui#98](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive-ui/pull/98), [omegahive-ui#102](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive-ui/pull/102).

## Added
1. Connectors: each person's secrets, sealed, reach only the minds they install them on. [omegahive#449](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive/pull/449).
2. Research: the full checked report is saved as a private artifact. [omegahive#768](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive/pull/768).
3. Updates: the hive shows the running release and the installer's update offer. [omegahive#270](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive/pull/270).
4. Mac: the hive starts again when its owner logs in, and an Omega Hive app in Applications opens it. [omegahive#282](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive/pull/282).
5. Ask: words sent while the mind answers show as queued, with Don't send. [omegahive-ui#105](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive-ui/pull/105).
6. Make: a page that declares a site shows Allow, and once allowed runs from the hive's address. [omegahive-ui#101](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive-ui/pull/101).

## Changed
1. Apps: an admin turns an app on only where no person owns the swarm, and off anywhere. [omegahive#751](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive/pull/751), [omegahive-ui#102](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive-ui/pull/102).
2. Apps: Where it runs offers Activate and Deactivate in the swarms a person owns, not only to admins; the Access panel offers activation and attachment only where the hive accepts them. [omegahive-ui#98](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive-ui/pull/98), [omegahive-ui#80](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive-ui/pull/80).
3. Ask: the model gets the person's current local date. [omegahive#769](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive/pull/769).
4. Ask shares personal memory with the owner's Studio maker, and the owner's Ask memory is kept when Studio answers. [omegahive#767](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive/pull/767), [omegahive-ui#251](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive-ui/pull/251).
5. Limits: a person's allowance leaves out what they spent on their own key. [omegahive#711](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive/pull/711).
6. Mind chat: a mind reads the linked folders it is permitted to read. [omegahive#770](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive/pull/770).
7. Research follows a hive's first admin without a restart. [omegahive#349](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive/pull/349).
8. Cosmos, Flux, Still, Construct and asks: a new mind gets no cap of its own, now also in the UI. [omegahive-ui#100](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive-ui/pull/100).
9. Payer: code edits and flow steps are checked when made, and a person's goal note and Remind go on their behalf. [omegahive#548](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive/pull/548).
10. Feedback: the log ring adds to hive.log instead of taking the hive's warnings from it. [omegahive#550](https://github.com/omega-flagship/omegahive/pull/550).
